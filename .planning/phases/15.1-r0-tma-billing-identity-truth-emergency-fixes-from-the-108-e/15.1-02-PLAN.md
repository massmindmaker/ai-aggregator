<objective>
Close the two identity-truth holes in the shipped TMA auth surface:

- **R0-4** — the JWT-verify middleware already overwrites `x-tma-user-id` from the verified `sub`, BUT on Next 14.2.15 the CVE-2025-29927 `x-middleware-subrequest` trick lets a request SKIP middleware entirely (re-enabling header spoof). Fix: bump Next.js to ≥14.2.33 AND strip `x-middleware-subrequest` at nginx (defense-in-depth). Also harden `jwtVerify` — pin `algorithms: ['HS256']` (block alg-confusion), bind `iss`/`aud`, and add a Redis-backed JWT denylist for revocation.
- **R0-5** — remove the `?? 'dev-only-change-in-prod'` fallback in BOTH `middleware.ts` and `auth/verify/route.ts`; fail hard (throw) if `TMA_JWT_SECRET` is unset, and assert length ≥ 32.

Purpose: make `x-tma-user-id` un-spoofable and remove the silent-bypass secret so no client can act as another user.
Output: a patched Next runtime, an HS256-pinned + denylist-checked middleware, a fail-hard secret on both auth surfaces, and the nginx directive that strips the bypass header.
</objective>
