The gap

Demand and developers are not.

A network of GPUs only earns its keep when real workloads flow through it. Gonka's own roadmap puts developer & AI-agent access as a priority track — because activation, not hardware, is the bottleneck.

▦Supply is ready

Why us

The lowest-friction path to RU inference demand.

  • We are an aggregator already — Track-2 P2 is our day job, not a new build. Gonka adds optionality, never becomes a dependency.
  • We bring distribution: a Telegram-native funnel into a RU-speaking agent-builder + user base. Where OpenAI-compat alone leaves builders, we hand them a two-tap on-ramp.
  • Our billing is metered to the call. Grants/credits tied to measurable usage — Gonka's stated model — map directly onto our ledger.
  • We honor BYO-wallet = zero commission. A user paying Gonka in GNK from their own wallet pays us nothing — pure demand to your network.

Honest scope: Gonka is one optional provider plus a grant. We keep our other paths primary — which is exactly why the demand we route to Gonka is real, durable, and not propped up by a single dependency.

aggregator + Telegram distribution + metered billing = real demand
