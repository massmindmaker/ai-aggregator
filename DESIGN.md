# AI Aggregator design contract

The live design source is `apps/web/src/app/globals.css` plus `apps/web/tailwind.config.ts`.

AI Aggregator uses a warm near-black/light neutral system with amber as the primary action accent. Inter is the body face; JetBrains Mono is used for prices, usage, IDs and other technical values. Prefer hairline borders and clear hierarchy over heavy shadows. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid blocking input.

Customer-facing copy is concise Russian. Payment, balance and quota states must include text as well as color. Never show an unfinished payment, provider or author workflow as available.

The former combined Web/TMA design contract is historical. Agents Market owns its web and TMA design under `/home/bob/Projects/agents-market`.
